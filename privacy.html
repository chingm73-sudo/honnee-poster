Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

Honnee Poster respects the privacy of its users. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when users connect and use our service.

Information We Access

When a user authorizes TikTok, Honnee Poster may access information permitted by the user and TikTok, such as basic account information and permissions required to publish content.

How We Use Information

Information is used only to provide the requested functionality, including connecting authorized accounts and publishing content requested by the user.

Data Sharing

Honnee Poster does not sell users' personal information. Information is only processed as necessary to provide the service or comply with applicable requirements.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information associated with the service.

User Control

Users may revoke Honnee Poster's access to their TikTok account through their TikTok account settings.

Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the Honnee Poster administrator.
